# Third-party assets

None. Everything in the 3D room is made in code:

- the seated Nepali characters, their clothes (daura-suruwal, kurta, sari, gunyu-cholo, shawl), Dhaka and
  Bhadgaule topis and jewellery: `src/ui/table/three/Villager.tsx`, `garments.ts`, `looks.ts`
- wood grain, table, plate, lamp, chairs and the card faces (from the game's own card art):
  `src/ui/table/three/textures.ts`, `Room.tsx`
- sound: synthesised with Web Audio in `src/ui/sound/sfx.ts`
