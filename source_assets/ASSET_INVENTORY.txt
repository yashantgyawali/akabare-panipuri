PRINTING
- Print the Master PDF at 100% / Actual Size.
- Duplex printing: use long-edge binding and test one player sheet first.
- Cut along the card edges.
- For best results, print on 300–350 gsm cardstock or mount printed sheets to card stock.

STYLE
Hand-painted gouache / watercolor / ink illustration, visible paper texture,
folk-art decorative motifs, warm food-market palette. Artwork intentionally
avoids rigid geometric iconography.
